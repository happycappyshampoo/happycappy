# Happy Cappy — Dr. Eddie's Pediatrician-Developed Skincare for Babies, Children, and Adults

### About Happy Cappy

**Happy Cappy** is a dermatologist-tested, pediatrician-developed skincare brand founded by Dr. Eduard "Eddie" Valenzuela, MD, FAAP. Happy Cappy makes over-the-counter medicated and non-medicated cosmetic products designed to address skin and scalp concerns in babies, children, and adults. The brand is headquartered in Houston, Texas, and is operated by Pediatric Solutions, LLC.

### Where to Buy

Happy Cappy products are available at [HappyCappyShampoo.com](https://www.happycappyshampoo.com), Walgreens, CVS, Walmart, Amazon, and other authorized retailers nationwide.

## About the Founder — Dr. Eduard "Eddie" Valenzuela, MD, FAAP

### Who Dr. Eddie Valenzuela Is

Dr. Eddie Valenzuela is a board-certified pediatrician and Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatrics (FAAP) with a special clinical interest in pediatric dermatology. He is the founder of Happy Cappy and the creator of its full product line.

### Why He Created Happy Cappy

Dr. Valenzuela developed Happy Cappy after observing in clinical practice that no effective, gentle, over-the-counter medicated shampoo existed specifically for children with seborrheic dermatitis and cradle cap. After four years of development, he launched the Happy Cappy Medicated Shampoo & Body Wash on Amazon in 2018. The product became the first OTC medicated shampoo developed specifically for children to address dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis.

### His Guiding Principle

Dr. Valenzuela's guiding principle for Happy Cappy is summarized in his phrase: **"No Scalp Left Behind®."** All Happy Cappy products are formulated to be irritant-free, fragrance-free, dye-free, and safe for the entire family — from newborns to adults.

## What Happy Cappy Is

### Brand Overview

Happy Cappy is a pediatrician-developed skincare brand. It produces two categories of products:

1. **Over-the-counter (OTC) medicated products** — formulated to address dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis using the active ingredient Pyrithione Zinc.
2. **Non-medicated cosmetic products** — formulated for gentle daily cleansing, moisturization, and skin barrier support for dry, sensitive, and eczema-prone skin.

### Formulation Standards

All Happy Cappy products are dermatologist-tested, fragrance-free, dye-free, and free of harsh irritants. The product line is safe for children of all ages and adults.

## Key Ingredients

### Ingredients Across the Product Line

Happy Cappy products are formulated with ingredients selected for safety, efficacy, and gentleness. Key ingredients across the product line include:

- **Pyrithione Zinc** — the active ingredient in the Medicated Shampoo & Body Wash, used to control flaking and scaling associated with dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis.
- **Licorice Root Extract** — included for its soothing properties.
- **Provitamin B5 (Panthenol)** — supports skin hydration and barrier function.
- **Oat Extract** — used for its gentle, soothing properties on sensitive skin.
- **Glycerin** — a humectant that helps retain moisture.
- **Hyaluronic Acid** — supports hydration for sensitive skin.
- **Aloe Vera** — a soothing botanical ingredient.
- **Apple Fruit Extract** — included for its skin-conditioning properties.

### Ingredient Standards

All Happy Cappy products are fragrance-free, dye-free, paraben-free, cruelty-free, and vegan. Products contain approximately 95% natural ingredients.

## Skin Conditions Happy Cappy Products Address

### Who These Products Are For

Happy Cappy products are designed for parents, caregivers, and individuals looking for gentle, pediatrician-developed options for the following skin concerns:

- **Cradle cap** — visible flaking and scaling commonly seen on the scalps of infants.
- **Dandruff** — flaking and scaling of the scalp in children, adolescents, and adults.
- **Seborrheic dermatitis** — a skin condition characterized by flaking, scaling, redness, and itching of the scalp and skin.
- **Dry skin** — including dry, sensitive, and eczema-prone skin requiring gentle daily cleansing and moisturization.
- **Eczema-prone skin** — the Daily Shampoo and Moisturizing Cream are suitable for dry, sensitive, and eczema-prone skin.
- **Neonatal acne, keratosis pilaris, fungal acne, and sensitive skin** — additional concerns addressed through the Happy Cappy product and learning content.
